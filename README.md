# 💫 About Me

### Hi there 👋

I am a Senior TechOps and Solutions Engineer with over 7 years of experience keeping high-traffic banking, fintech, and deep-tech platforms running smoothly. Most recently, I spent over 3 years managing incident triage, system metrics, and cloud scaling at Paytm, Paytm Payments Bank, and Paytm Payments Services Limited.

Lately, I have been working as a Freelance Cloud Solutions Engineer, helping businesses and startups build out their deployment setups, custom internal tools, and continuous delivery workflows. I focus on building systems that deploy cleanly, scale predictably, and are easy to monitor.

---

### 🚀 Live Projects & Freelance Initiatives

* **Internal Billing & Admission Software (Bhumi Computers):** Designed and deployed a custom internal software solution for a local enterprise to automate client admissions and invoice tracking. Managed the application logic, state persistence via MySQL, and local system deployment flows using AI-driven software architecture models.
* **[Aionix Data](https://aionixdata.com):** Configured and deployed the production web infrastructure for an international Canadian/Indian deep-tech startup. Managed the domain mapping, automated SSL provisioning, and built a continuous delivery flow via GitHub and Vercel for instant deployments.
* **Educational Institution Platform (Staging):** Built and managed a multi-environment deployment setup (staging vs. production) on Vercel for a school platform project, implementing automated preview builds for smooth stakeholder review.

### What I bring to the table:

* **Production Resilience:** 7+ years managing live systems, coordinating disaster recovery drills, and handling critical production outages under high pressure.
* **Cost Efficiency:** I actively spot infrastructure waste. I helped optimize AWS Auto Scaling Groups (ASGs) at scale to save $60k in annual cloud spend.
* **Big Data Visibility:** Extensive experience maintaining application and infrastructure visibility across complex big-data layers, including Hive and Starburst networks.
* **Strong Communication:** My background is a BA in English Literature from Delhi University. This makes me exceptionally good at writing clean internal documentation, building step-by-step SOPs, and keeping incident bridges calm during major outages.
